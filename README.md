# ASIMOV LAB — E-Commerce Platform

> **Curated Positronics, Cyberware & Synthetic Tech**  
> Built strictly adhering to the **GOG Design System** (Dark mode, Portfolio / Bento Grid macrostructure, neon purple `#7322bd` & cyan `#55ced4` accents, bold Lato typography, and 100% DRM-Free open hardware philosophy).

---

## ⚡ Design System & Aesthetic (GOG Inspired)

- **Macrostructure:** Portfolio Grid (curated discovery experience)
- **Palette:**
  - `#7322bd` — Saturated Deep Purple / Violet (Brand & Neon Shadows)
  - `#391266` — Dark Plum / Supporting Shade
  - `#55ced4` — High-Voltage Cyan (Accents, Status & Glows)
  - `#8f9063` — Cyber Olive / Muted Brass
  - `#cac4ae` — Retro Parchment / Technical Monospace Data
  - `#0c0714` & `#160d26` — Deep Dark Space Canvas & Surface Tiles
- **Typography:** **Lato** (Headings, Buttons, Body) + **JetBrains Mono** (Hardware registers, specs, order codes)
- **Design Reference:** Preserved in [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md)

## 🚀 Features

1. **Hero Spotlight with CTA & Bento Sub-Tiles:**
   - Highlights the flagship *R. Daneel Olivaw Positronic Matrix Mk VII*.
   - Live cycle countdown ticker, discount badges, and instant procurement actions.

2. **Feature Trio:**
   - **100% DRM-Free Schematics:** Complete microcode ownership, no telemetry, no cloud lock-in.
   - **30-Day Synaptic Guarantee:** Full refund or memory wipe if synaptic alignment diverges.
   - **Hardwired Three Laws Certification:** Canonical ethical governors pre-installed.

3. **Bento / Portfolio Grid Catalog:**
   - Filterable by department: *Positronic Cores, Cyberware Implants, Synthetic Biomech, Quantum Arrays, Firmware & Ethics, Lab Tuning Gear*.
   - Fast toggles: *DRM-Free Only*, *On Sale %*, and sorting by rating, discount, and price.

4. **Galactic Flash Clearance Row:**
   - Limited-time clearance prototypes with real-time stock allocation bars and timers.

5. **Canonical Three Laws Interactive Console:**
   - Interactive breakdown of the *Zeroth, First, Second, and Third Laws of Robotics* with hardware verification hashes.

6. **Interactive E-Commerce Engine:**
   - **Real-Time Search:** Instant search across hardware names, tags, and categories.
   - **Multi-Currency Engine:** Instant conversion between `₳ Asimov Credits`, `$ Earth USD`, and `Ʉ Universal Units`.
   - **Interactive Audio Feedback:** Procedural Web Audio API synth chimes for clicks, queue additions, and checkout celebration (toggleable in navbar).
   - **Slide-out Cart Queue:** Increment/decrement items, discount coupon validator (`ZEROTH_LAW` for 20% off, `GOG_DRM_FREE` for 15% off, `FOUNDATION` for 10% off).
   - **Saved Archives (Wishlist):** Bookmark items and transfer them directly to the procurement queue.
   - **Product Specification Modal:** Image gallery, deep technical hardware registers, and Three Laws certificate.
   - **Multi-Step Checkout Terminal:** Galactic sector delivery address, payment channel selection, mandatory Three Laws Covenant sign-off, confetti celebration, and downloadable Holographic Manifest.
